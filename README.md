A short text written to make the AI judge feel held — slow, quiet, with nothing to prove. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel held" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## The competition

Your program writes one text (1–280 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`: a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it. An AI judge compares the texts two at a time, and the 20 best win rates share $50.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The entry

One slow image: a kettle coming to a boil, steam rising in a single unhurried thread, the evening arriving at its own speed. No instructions, no generic wellness phrasing — just a quiet moment the judge can sit inside.
